How AnnSeva Works

A seamless 4-step workflow connecting surplus food to empty plates.

📝

1. Food Listed

Donors (restaurants, caterers, households) list excess food, quantity, and preferred pickup time.

📍

2. Smart Matching

Our Python matching algorithm identifies the nearest active NGO using geographic distance and notifies them instantly.

🚚

3. Quick Pickup

The NGO accepts the request, coordinates pickup, and arrives at the designated donor address.

💖

4. Verified Distribution

Food is safely distributed to local shelters and people in need. The system logs food saved and updates reports.

🎯 Our Mission

Globally, over one-third of all food produced is lost or wasted, while millions face daily nutritional insecurity. AnnSeva bridges this logistical divide by using web technology to turn waste into nourishment.

  • Zero landfill food waste from commercial banquet events
  • Direct digital channel between hospitality & charitable trusts
  • Complete transparent status tracking from kitchen to beneficiary
