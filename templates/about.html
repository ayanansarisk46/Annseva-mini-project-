About AnnSeva System

Web-based Food Donation and Management System

🚨 The Problem

Food waste is a major problem across our cities. Hotels, restaurants, caterers, banquet halls, and event hosts frequently produce surplus cooked meals and edible groceries. Without an organized digital system, this nutritious food ends up in municipal landfills, while thousands of people in nearby slums and shelters suffer from hunger.

Traditional food donation happens ad-hoc via sporadic phone calls. Food donors do not know which NGOs are active nearby or have vehicles available, causing food to spoil before it can be collected.

🎯 Project Objectives

  • Reduce Food Waste: Divert edible surplus food from waste bins to charitable organizations.
  • Fast Matchmaking: Match food donors with nearest suitable NGOs using Python proximity matching.
  • Workflow Tracking: Track the donation lifecycle across 4 distinct states: PENDING → ACCEPTED → PICKED UP → COMPLETED.
  • In-App Alerts: Keep Donors and NGOs updated in real-time regarding acceptance, pickup, and completion.
  • Measurable Impact: Generate dynamic charts (Chart.js) reporting total kilograms saved and meals served.

💻 Technology Stack

Frontend:
  • HTML5 & CSS3
  • Vanilla JavaScript
  • Chart.js (Visual Reports)
Backend:
  • Python 3
  • Flask Web Framework
  • Flask-Login & Werkzeug
Database:
  • MySQL Database
  • Flask-SQLAlchemy ORM
  • Auto SQLite fallback
Location & Logic:
  • Google Maps JS API
  • Leaflet.js Fallback
  • Haversine Proximity Match
