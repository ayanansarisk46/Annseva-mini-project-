💡 How Proximity Matching Works in AnnSeva

When a donor submits food, our Python backend analyzes the pickup locality, area, and city keywords against all registered NGO operating zones using Locality Proximity Matching. The request is immediately assigned to the closest NGO team in that district, minimizing transit time and keeping hot meals fresh.
